PM Remote

Reads a LATITUDE Summary page, flags values outside your thresholds and fills your remote-transmission template. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere and no AI is used.

Set up once

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto it:
    PM Remote
  3. On a patient's LATITUDE Summary page, click PM Remote. A panel opens with the flags and your filled report.

After changing the thresholds below, drag the button again and delete the old one: the settings are built into it.

Or paste the page

On the LATITUDE Summary page press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C, and paste here.
